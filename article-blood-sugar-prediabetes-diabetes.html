Blood Sugar, Prediabetes and Diabetes Explained

Medical author: Dr. Farhan Ahmed Khan
Published: 27 August 2026

Glucose is an important fuel for the body. Blood glucose levels are regulated mainly through insulin and other hormones. When regulation becomes impaired, blood tests may show prediabetes or diabetes.

Key point: One abnormal result does not always establish a diagnosis. Symptoms, test type and repeat or confirmatory testing can matter.

What is prediabetes?

Prediabetes describes blood glucose that is above the usual range but does not meet diagnostic criteria for diabetes. It is an important opportunity to address weight, physical activity, diet and other cardiovascular risk factors.

What is diabetes?

Diabetes is a group of conditions characterized by persistent problems with glucose regulation. Type 1 and type 2 diabetes have different mechanisms, and treatment should be individualized.

Why does cardiovascular health matter?

Diabetes often coexists with high blood pressure, abnormal lipids and excess body weight. Addressing the overall risk profile can be as important as focusing on a single glucose value.
