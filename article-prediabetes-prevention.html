Prediabetes: Causes, Risks and Prevention

Medical author: Dr. Farhan Ahmed Khan
Published: 27 August 2026

Prediabetes is common and often has no obvious symptoms. It signals increased risk of future type 2 diabetes and may coexist with other cardiovascular risk factors.

Key point: Prediabetes is not inevitable progression to diabetes. Meaningful risk reduction is possible, especially when action is taken early.

Common risk factors

Risk can be influenced by family history, excess body weight, low physical activity, previous gestational diabetes, age and other metabolic factors.

Prevention priorities

  • Build regular physical activity into the week.
  • Improve dietary quality with minimally processed foods, vegetables, legumes and appropriate portions.
  • Address weight when clinically appropriate and achievable.
  • Prioritize adequate sleep and avoid tobacco.
  • Monitor blood pressure and lipid risk alongside glucose.

When should you seek assessment?

Discuss testing if you have risk factors, a previous abnormal glucose result, symptoms of significant hyperglycaemia or a strong family history.
