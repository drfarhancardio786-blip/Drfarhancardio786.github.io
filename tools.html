Blood Pressure Analyzer

Understand a blood-pressure reading

Enter an adult resting blood-pressure reading for a general educational interpretation.

Enter both numbers to see an interpretation.

For a better reading

  • Rest quietly for about 5 minutes before measuring.
  • Use a validated upper-arm cuff of the appropriate size.
  • Keep the arm supported at heart level and avoid talking during the measurement.
  • A single reading does not establish a diagnosis.

Educational tool for adults only. Thresholds can differ between guidelines and clinical settings. This is not a diagnosis. If blood pressure is extremely high or is accompanied by chest pain, severe shortness of breath, neurological symptoms, confusion, or fainting, seek urgent medical care.
