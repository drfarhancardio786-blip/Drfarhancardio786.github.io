🥗 Simple Nutrition Guide

A practical starting point for many adults is to make roughly half the plate vegetables and fruit, include a protein source, choose whole or minimally processed carbohydrate sources, and use unsaturated fats in appropriate amounts.

  • Prioritize vegetables, fruit and fibre-rich foods.
  • Include adequate protein across the day.
  • Limit highly processed foods, excess sodium and added sugars.
  • Choose water as the main beverage for most occasions.

Nutrition needs vary with age, medical conditions, pregnancy, activity and other factors.
