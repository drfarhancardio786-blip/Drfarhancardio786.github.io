🩸 Blood Pressure Analyzer

Enter an adult resting blood-pressure reading.

Enter systolic and diastolic values.

For a more reliable measurement

  • Rest quietly for about 5 minutes beforehand.
  • Use an appropriately sized upper-arm cuff.
  • Keep your arm supported at heart level.
  • Avoid talking during the measurement.
  • Consider repeated readings rather than relying on one measurement.

Safety: A reading of 180/120 mmHg or higher is very high. Repeat it after a short rest. If it remains this high, seek prompt medical advice. If it is accompanied by chest pain, severe shortness of breath, weakness/numbness, difficulty speaking, confusion, fainting or other severe symptoms, seek emergency medical care.

Educational information for adults. BP thresholds can differ between guidelines and clinical settings. This tool is not a diagnosis and does not replace professional medical assessment.
