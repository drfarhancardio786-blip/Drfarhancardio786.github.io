How to Start Exercising Safely

Regular physical activity can improve cardiovascular fitness, strength, mood and metabolic health. Starting gradually is generally more sustainable than trying to do too much too quickly.

Start small: A manageable routine that you can repeat is usually better than an ambitious plan you abandon.

A simple framework

  1. Choose an activity you can repeat, such as walking.
  2. Begin with a comfortable duration and progress gradually.
  3. Add strength exercises for major muscle groups as fitness improves.
  4. Allow recovery when needed.

Pay attention to symptoms

Stop exercise and seek appropriate medical advice for concerning symptoms such as chest pressure or pain, unexplained fainting, severe breathlessness, or a new sustained irregular or very rapid heartbeat. People with known cardiovascular disease or substantial limitations may need individualized advice before a new program.

Intensity

At moderate effort, breathing and heart rate increase while conversation remains possible. Maximal intensity is not required for exercise to be beneficial.
