HEART HEALTH

Take care of your heart

Learn about cardiovascular risk, blood pressure, exercise and prevention.

Blood pressure

Measure it properly

Rest quietly, use a validated cuff of the right size, support the arm and repeat measurements when appropriate.

Fitness

Move for your heart

Regular aerobic activity and strength training can support cardiovascular health when appropriate for the individual.

Prevention

Reduce modifiable risk

Smoking, inactivity, unhealthy diet, excess weight, high blood pressure and abnormal lipids can all influence cardiovascular risk.
