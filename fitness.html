FITNESS

Build fitness that lasts

Practical principles for cardio, strength, mobility and sustainable exercise.

Build fitness gradually

A useful starting point is to make exercise regular and manageable rather than trying to do too much too soon.

Cardio

Walking, cycling, swimming and similar activities can build endurance. Increase duration and intensity gradually.

Strength

Resistance exercise supports muscle strength and function. Begin with manageable loads and sound technique.

Mobility

Regular movement through comfortable ranges of motion can complement aerobic and resistance training.
