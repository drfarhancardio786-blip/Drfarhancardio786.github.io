How to Lower LDL Cholesterol

Medical author: Dr. Farhan Ahmed Khan
Published: 25 August 2026

LDL cholesterol is one important contributor to atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. The best approach depends on the LDL level, overall cardiovascular risk and whether cardiovascular disease is already present.

Build a heart-healthy eating pattern

Emphasize vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains and other minimally processed foods. Replacing foods high in saturated fat with healthier unsaturated fat sources can help improve LDL cholesterol for many people.

Be physically active

Regular physical activity supports cardiovascular health, metabolic health and weight management. Choose activity that is appropriate for your health status and gradually build consistency.

Address smoking and other risk factors

Avoiding tobacco and controlling blood pressure, diabetes and other modifiable risk factors can reduce overall cardiovascular risk even though each intervention may affect different pathways.

What about medication?

Medication may be recommended for some people based on LDL cholesterol and overall cardiovascular risk. Statins and other lipid-lowering medicines should be considered through individualized clinical assessment rather than self-treatment.
