Understanding blood pressure

A beginner-friendly guide to what blood pressure numbers represent and why repeated measurements matter.

What do the numbers mean?

Blood pressure is usually reported as systolic pressure over diastolic pressure. The systolic value reflects pressure when the heart contracts; the diastolic value reflects pressure between beats.

Why one reading is not the whole story

Blood pressure varies with activity, stress, caffeine, pain, sleep and measurement technique. Repeated, properly performed measurements can provide a more useful picture than a single reading.

Better measurement habits

  • Rest quietly before measuring.
  • Use an appropriately sized cuff.
  • Keep the arm supported and roughly at heart level.
  • Take repeated readings when advised and record them.

When to seek help

Very high readings accompanied by severe symptoms such as chest pain, severe breathlessness, neurological symptoms or collapse require urgent medical assessment.
