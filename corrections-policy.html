Corrections & Updates Policy

How Vitanora handles factual corrections and important content updates.

Our commitment

Health information can change as evidence, professional guidance and clinical understanding develop. Vitanora aims to correct meaningful factual errors and update important content when material information becomes outdated.

How to report a possible error

Please contact us with the page title or URL, the statement you believe may be inaccurate, and—if available—a reputable source that supports your concern. Reports can be sent through the Contact page.

How reports are assessed

Reports are reviewed in context. Where a correction is warranted, the relevant content may be revised, clarified, qualified or removed. Not every disagreement requires a correction; medical questions can involve uncertainty or differences in interpretation, so changes should reflect the best available evidence and the purpose of an educational page.

Content updates

Pages may be updated when important clinical guidance changes, when better evidence becomes available, or when clearer wording improves reader safety and understanding. When practical, article pages should display a meaningful publication or update date.

Transparency and limitations

Vitanora does not promise that every page will reflect every new publication immediately. Readers should use current professional guidance and appropriate clinical care for time-sensitive or individual medical decisions.

Editorial independence

Corrections and updates are intended to improve accuracy and clarity rather than promote a commercial interest. Learn more in the Editorial Policy.
