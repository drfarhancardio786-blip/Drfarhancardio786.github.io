The basics of a balanced plate

A simple framework for making everyday meals more nutritious without counting every gram.

Think in food groups

A balanced meal can emphasize vegetables and fruit, include an adequate protein source, and add high-fibre carbohydrate foods and healthy fats according to individual needs.

Choose mostly minimally processed foods

Whole or minimally processed foods can make it easier to obtain fibre, micronutrients and adequate protein. Processed foods are not automatically unhealthy, but heavily refined foods can be easy to overconsume.

Make it sustainable

The best eating pattern is one you can maintain. Cultural foods, budget, schedule, preferences and medical needs should all be considered.
