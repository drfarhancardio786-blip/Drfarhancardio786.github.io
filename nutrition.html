Eat well without overcomplicating it

Simple nutrition principles for balanced, sustainable everyday eating.

Keep nutrition practical

Healthy eating does not require perfection. A useful framework is to emphasize minimally processed foods, vegetables and fruit, adequate protein, whole grains or other high-fibre carbohydrates, and mostly unsaturated fats.
