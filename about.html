About Vitanora

Vitanora is a health and fitness education platform designed to make useful health information and simple tools easier to understand and use.

Explore ArticlesExplore Health Tools

Our Mission

Our aim is to present practical, understandable information about health, cardiovascular wellness, fitness, nutrition and prevention in a format that is useful in everyday life.

Evidence-informed

Medical topics should be based on established clinical knowledge and reputable health guidance. Where appropriate, articles can provide references so readers can explore the underlying evidence.

Practical

Vitanora focuses on clear explanations, practical habits and simple educational tools rather than unnecessary complexity.

Our Tools

Vitanora includes educational calculators and analyzers such as BMI, blood pressure, BMR, target heart rate and waist-to-height ratio. These tools provide estimates and are not intended to diagnose disease.

Medical Disclaimer

Vitanora provides general health education and does not replace evaluation, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional. Individual medical decisions should be made with an appropriate clinician who knows the person's history and circumstances.

Emergency symptoms should never be managed using an online article or calculator. Seek appropriate urgent or emergency medical care when necessary.

Editorial Principles

  • Use clear, accessible language.
  • Distinguish educational information from individualized medical advice.
  • Prefer reputable clinical and public-health sources.
  • Update health information when important guidance changes.
  • Present uncertainty honestly rather than overstating claims.

Contact
