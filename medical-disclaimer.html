Medical Disclaimer

Please read before relying on Vitanora articles or tools.

Vitanora provides general health education only. The website does not establish a doctor-patient relationship and does not provide individualized diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.

Not a substitute for clinical care

Health decisions depend on personal history, examination, investigations, medications and other factors that a general website cannot assess. Discuss personal health concerns with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Educational tools

Calculators and analyzers provide estimates based on the information entered and the formulas or rules used. Results may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for a particular individual and should not be used as the sole basis for medical decisions.

Emergency symptoms

Do not delay emergency care while reading an article or using an online tool. Symptoms such as severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, new weakness, trouble speaking, sudden confusion or other serious symptoms require urgent professional assessment.

Accuracy and updates

Vitanora aims to provide accurate and current information, but medical knowledge and guidance can change. No website can guarantee that every item is complete or appropriate for every situation.
