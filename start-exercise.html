How to start exercising safely

A practical framework for moving from little activity to a sustainable routine.

Start below your maximum

For many beginners, consistency is more important than intensity. Begin with an amount of activity you can recover from comfortably and repeat regularly.

Build three components

  • Aerobic activity: walking, cycling, swimming or similar activities.
  • Strength: manageable resistance exercises for major muscle groups.
  • Mobility: comfortable movement through useful ranges of motion.

Progress gradually

Increase duration, frequency or intensity progressively rather than changing everything at once. Pain, unusual breathlessness, fainting or concerning chest symptoms should not simply be pushed through.
