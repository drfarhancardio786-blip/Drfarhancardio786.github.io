Metabolic Syndrome and Cardiovascular Risk

Medical author: Dr. Farhan Ahmed Khan
Published: 27 August 2026

Metabolic syndrome describes a pattern of interconnected metabolic risk factors. Definitions vary slightly between organizations, but the central concept is that several abnormalities can occur together and increase future cardiometabolic risk.

Key point: Risk is cumulative. Blood pressure, glucose, triglycerides, HDL cholesterol, body composition and lifestyle should be considered together.

Why does it develop?

Insulin resistance, excess adiposity, genetics, diet, low physical activity and other environmental factors can contribute.

Why does it matter?

The combination of metabolic abnormalities is associated with increased risk of type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease.

What can improve the overall pattern?

Sustainable physical activity, improved dietary quality, weight management when appropriate, treatment of hypertension or lipid disorders and diabetes prevention can all be important parts of care.
