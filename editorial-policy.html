Editorial Policy

How Vitanora develops, reviews and updates health education.

Our purpose

Vitanora provides general, practical health, fitness, nutrition and prevention education. Our goal is to explain established health knowledge clearly without presenting general information as a substitute for individualized clinical care.

Medical authorship and review

Important medical content should identify its author or contributor where appropriate. The medical author and founder is described on the Medical Author page. Content created or medically reviewed by a qualified professional should be described accurately, without overstating credentials or certainty.

Evidence and sources

We aim to base health claims on established clinical knowledge, reputable public-health organizations, professional guidelines and appropriate scientific literature. Important claims should be supported by references where useful. Evidence can change, and uncertainty should be communicated honestly.

Editorial independence

Editorial decisions are made independently and are intended to prioritize accuracy, clarity and reader safety over commercial promotion.

Corrections and updates

If a meaningful factual error is identified, Vitanora aims to correct or clarify it. Content may also be updated when important guidance or evidence changes. Read the Corrections & Updates Policy for more detail.

Use of digital tools

Digital tools may assist with drafting, organization or presentation, but medical claims should be checked for accuracy and supported by appropriate evidence before publication.

Reader safety

General education cannot account for an individual's complete medical history. Articles and tools should distinguish educational information from diagnosis and should encourage appropriate professional or emergency care when symptoms or circumstances require it.

Contact

Readers can report possible factual errors or provide feedback through the Contact page.
