Overview

Vitanora is an informational website. This policy explains the basic approach to information that may be processed when you use the site.

Vitanora's educational calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. Do not enter unnecessary personally identifying or sensitive medical information into general educational tools.

Contact information

If you contact Vitanora by email, the information you voluntarily provide may be used to read and respond to your message. Please avoid sending confidential medical records or information that requires urgent clinical assessment.

Analytics and third parties

Vitanora may use standard hosting, search and analytics services as the website develops. Those services may process technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited or IP-related data according to their own policies.

External links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience or reference. Vitanora is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website's features change. The date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact
