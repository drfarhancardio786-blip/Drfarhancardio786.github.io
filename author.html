MEDICAL AUTHOR

Dr. Farhan Ahmed Khan

Medical author and founder of Vitanora.

Professional background

Dr. Farhan Ahmed Khan is the medical author and creator behind Vitanora, an independent health and fitness education platform focused on clear, practical and evidence-informed information.

Credentials: MBBS, MSCVmed, MD Cardiology, CCT PIMS, MPhil Cardiovascular Research, Member ACC.

Areas of focus

  • Cardiovascular health and prevention
  • Blood pressure and home monitoring
  • Cholesterol and cardiovascular risk
  • Preventive health and healthy lifestyle education
  • Clear communication of evidence-informed medical information

Role at Vitanora

Dr. Khan develops and reviews Vitanora's medical education with the aim of translating established health knowledge into accessible information. Vitanora does not provide individualized diagnosis or treatment through its articles or tools.

Editorial standards

Health information should be transparent about its purpose and limitations. Vitanora aims to identify medical authorship, use reputable evidence where appropriate, update material when important guidance changes and correct meaningful factual errors.
